Design Principles (Lab 3.2)

Good Website Design

This website exemplifies a good website:

  • Easy navigation with links at top of the screen
  • Uniform and clean layout
  • Consistent colour palette across all pages and high contrast text and readable fonts

Bad Website Design

This website exemplifies a bad website:

  • Lacks consistency in terms of overall theme, and colour
  • Low readability due to poor contrast and overwhelming content
